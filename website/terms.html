Using ITER

Last updated 19 October 2026 · By using the app or this website, you agree to these terms

The short version

  • ITER is a student prototype. It may change, have mistakes or stop working.
  • Its numbers are estimates to help you plan, not financial or tax advice.
  • Always check what the AI read before you save it.
  • No real money is charged in this prototype.

What ITER is

ITER is a travel money prototype built by Monash University students for a FinTech group project. It is provided as it is, for testing and demonstration. Features, prices and limits may change, and the service may be paused or ended at any time.

Safe to spend and other estimates

The "safe to spend" figure, spending warnings and trip summaries are calculated from what you enter and what ITER reads from your receipts. They are a planning aid, not financial advice. You are responsible for your own spending decisions.

AI scanning

ITER uses AI to read receipts and menus. AI can misread text, prices, dates or currencies. Check every scan before you save it, and fix anything that looks wrong. Menu translations and the pork and alcohol flags are a guide only. If you have a dietary, religious or allergy need, confirm with the restaurant.

Tax refunds

ITER checks receipts against published tourist tax-free rules. Japan is supported, based on the National Tax Agency of Japan's guide, last checked on 15 October 2026. The refund amounts shown are estimates. Whether you get a refund is decided by the shop, the refund operator and customs when you leave the country, and rules can change.

Exchange rates

Exchange rates are daily reference rates and are shown for guidance. Your bank, card or money changer will use its own rate and fees.

Plans and passes

  • Prices on this website are the planned prices. In the prototype, buying a pass is simulated and no money is charged.
  • The free trial covers the first 3 days of your trip, with up to 10 scans a day.
  • A pass starts by itself on your next trip day that isn't already covered. A pass you never start expires 12 months after purchase.
  • Without a pass, Community, trip planning and your own records stay free.

Community

Be respectful and keep it about travel and spending. Don't post anyone else's personal information, and don't post anything illegal, hateful or misleading. You can delete your own posts, and you can report or block others. In the prototype, other travellers' posts are samples.

As far as the law allows, the ITER team is not responsible for losses caused by relying on ITER's estimates, scans, rates or refund checks. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under Malaysian consumer law.
