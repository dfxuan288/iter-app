Privacy notice

Your trip stays on your phone

Last updated 19 October 2026 · Applies to the ITER app and this website

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your trips, expenses, photos and community activity are saved on your own phone, not on our servers.
  • When you scan a receipt or menu, the photo is sent to be read by AI and is not kept.
  • No ads, no tracking across other sites, and we never sell data.

Who we are

ITER is a prototype built by a team of students at Monash University for a FinTech group project. It is not a registered company and is not offered commercially. This notice explains, in plain language, what the prototype does with information, following the principles of Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

What stays on your phone

ITER saves these in your phone's browser storage. We cannot see them:

  • Your trip: destination, dates, budget and category budgets
  • Your expenses, and the receipt and menu photos you take
  • Your tax refund tracker and your plan or pass status
  • Your community posts, comments, likes and the people you block
In this prototype, community posts from other travellers are sample posts. What you post is saved only on your phone and is not shown to anyone else.

Who helps us run ITER

  • Vercel hosts the app, this website and our server (Singapore region).
  • Anthropic provides the Claude AI that reads scanned photos.
  • Supabase stores the scan counter, the scan results and the exchange-rate cache.
  • Frankfurter supplies exchange rates.

Some of these services process data outside Malaysia. We share only what is listed above.

Payments

Passes in the prototype are simulated. No money is charged and ITER never asks for card or bank details.

This website

This website may count visits using Vercel Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not identify individual visitors. It shows us totals, such as how many people opened a page.

Your choices

  • Delete a trip: deleting a trip also deletes its expenses and receipt photos.
  • Start over: in the app, open Me and choose Reset the demo. This removes every trip, expense and community post you created.
  • Remove everything: to wipe all ITER data from your phone, including photos, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Delete a post: open your post in Community and choose Delete my post.
  • Don't scan: you can type expenses in by hand. Nothing is sent when you do.

Because your records live on your phone, we cannot see, recover or hand them over. If you clear them, they are gone.

Changes and questions

If what ITER does with information changes, we will update this page and its date. For questions about this notice, speak to the ITER team (Monash University FinTech group project).

This notice is written for a student prototype and is not legal advice. A public launch would need a registered company, a proper data-protection review and a dedicated contact for privacy requests.
